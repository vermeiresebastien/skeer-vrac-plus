6. FACTORY AUDIO — no third-party sample rights involved
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The six factory WAVs baked into the bundle (foley, kitchen, body, scrap,
circuit, vinyl: 73.9 s total, 16-bit) are generated in this repository by the
synthesis recipes in src/audio/sampleLibrary.ts:597-635 — 24 event recipes on a
fixed 0.47-0.64 s grid, with a fixed seed per source — and committed as baked
files under native/Resources/library/. They are not taken from any sample
library, so no third-party sample licence applies to them. [docs/next-steps-plan.md:1773-1777]

The SKEER wordmark (native/Resources/branding/skeer-wordmark.png) is SKEER's own.
